Title: Printer Not Printing

Problem:
A printer is connected to the computer but documents are not printing.

Possible Causes:
- Printer is offline
- Printer is out of paper
- Low or empty ink or toner
- Incorrect printer selected
- Print queue is stuck
- Printer driver problem
- Network connection problem

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Check whether the printer is powered on.
2. Check whether the printer displays an error message.
3. Make sure paper is loaded correctly.
4. Check the ink or toner level.
5. Confirm that the correct printer is selected.
6. Check whether the printer is shown as online.
7. Clear any stuck documents from the print queue.
8. Restart the printer.
9. Restart the computer.
10. Check whether the printer driver is installed correctly.

Escalation:
If the printer continues to show hardware errors after basic troubleshooting, contact the hardware or printer support team.
